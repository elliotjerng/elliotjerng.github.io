## About Me

I am a Master's student at [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/){:target="\_blank"} studying Neural Engineering. This spring, I graduated from [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/){:target="\_blank"} with a BS in Neuroscience and Data Science. I'm strongly interested in understanding information processing in neural systems.

Previously, I was a research assistant at Harvard Medical School in [Dr. Bernardo Sabatini's lab](https://sabatini.hms.harvard.edu/){:target="\_blank"}, working under the direction of Shun Li, a PhD candidate. We used optogenetics and operant learning to study synaptic sign switching in a population of co-releasing neurons. In support of the behavioral experiments, we developed several in silico models to investigate the advantages of the co-release mechanism.

I also gained valuable industry research experience as a Research Intern at [AbbVie](https://www.abbvie.com/){:target="\_blank"}, where I developed target validation assays and software to measure neural activity and identify schizophrenia-relevant phenotypes.
